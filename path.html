The initiative behind this course

PATH — Pathways for AI Training & Hiring.

This course is part of PATH, a national initiative led by MIT RAISE and Georgia State University that connects research universities, community colleges, industry, and government to expand practical, industry-aligned AI training and career pathways.

At a glance

What PATH is

Announced in April 2026, PATH is a multi-year effort to scale affordable, industry-aligned AI training — with a particular focus on turning 2- and 4-year colleges into engines of an AI-enabled workforce. Unlike large-scale online training efforts, PATH emphasizes in-person, collaborative, project-based learning: students work in teams on real problems brought by industry partners.

The model

How PATH works

State hubs

Universities + community colleges

Each hub pairs a research university with community colleges to design curricula matched to regional workforce needs. Georgia partners include Georgia Gwinnett College, Georgia State’s Perimeter College, and Clark Atlanta University.

Learning by doing

Real problems, real teams

Collaborative, project-based learning where students work in teams on challenges sourced from industry partners — the same philosophy behind this course’s build-and-break labs.

Into jobs

Credentials employers read

Clearer pathways from AI learning to real job opportunities through industry-informed micro-credentials, combining technical foundations with the professional skills employers ask for.
