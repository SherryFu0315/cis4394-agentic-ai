Agentic AI courses elsewhere.

Universities started teaching agents as a subject of its own in 2024, and most of the good courses publish their syllabi, slides and sometimes lectures. This page collects the ones with a public website, says what each emphasizes, and notes how this course differs. Every link was opened and checked on the date above; course sites move, so tell me if one breaks.

How to read this list. Three families, in practice. Systems and research courses (Stanford CS 329Z, CMU 11-768, Harvard CS 2680) assume a CS background and go down to training, serving and benchmarks. Builder courses (Berkeley's MOOC, UVA, MIT's studio, Stanford CS 146S) are project-driven and lighter on theory. Business-school courses (NYU Stern, and this one) start from the decision a manager has to make and work back to the mechanism. If you want to go deeper after Week 14, the systems courses are where to go; if you want more practice, the builder courses.
University courses with public websites · 2025–2026

Thirteen weeks is one path through the subject. Here are others.

Stanford · CS 329Z · Fall 2026

Engineering AI Agents

Diyi Yang, Michael Ryan, John Yang

The shift from single models to compound AI systems: students build RAG, tool use and the agent loop from scratch, then study how frameworks such as DSPy abstract them. Distinctive lectures: data for agentic systems (traces, flywheels, synthetic data), optimization (prompt optimization vs weights), LLM-as-judge and evaluation infrastructure, and proactive agents. Two applied homeworks and a quarter-long project; the first homework is a public evaluation harness.

Carnegie Mellon · 11-768 · Fall 2026

AI Agents

Daniel Fried, Graham Neubig (Language Technologies Institute)

LLM-based agents that perceive, reason, plan and act over many steps. The arc is: build a harness, build the evals for it, then train an agentic model with reinforcement learning; the second half is a research project with a poster session. Covers coding, GUI and deep-research agents, training (SFT, RL, systems), safety and interaction. Neubig maintains OpenHands, so the coding-agent material is first-hand.

UC Berkeley · CS 294/194-196 · Fall 2025 (MOOC)

Agentic AI

Dawn Song; Xinyun Chen (co-instructor); weekly guest lecturers

The third iteration of Berkeley RDI's open course (after LLM Agents, Fall 2024, and Advanced LLM Agents, Spring 2025). A lecture series by people who build the systems: OpenAI, NVIDIA, Sony AI and others. Free to follow online with recordings; the companion AgentX competition is open to the public. The best single place to hear the industry's own framing of the field, and the least structured as a course.

Harvard · CS 2680 · Fall 2026

Modern AI Systems: Agents and System Optimizations

Juncheng Yang (MaDSys group)

Follows one agentic system from the agent loop down through the serving stack: students first use a modern coding agent (Claude Code), then design their own agent with provided API access, measure it, and optimize the systems underneath. Five assignments with a public leaderboard and a final project. Prerequisites are real (a graduate systems course, PyTorch); anyone may audit the materials.

NYU Stern · Spring 2026 (second half)

Foundations of AI Agents

Srikanth Jagabathula, Ilan Lobel

The closest cousin to this course: a business school, no formal prerequisites, hands-on from day one. Six sessions (building an agent; agent architecture; the power of agentic AI; enterprise agents and evaluation; deploying agents, with an industry panel; demo day). Students ship a working agent on an n8n backend with a front end, submit weekly one-page deliverables, and peer-review two demos. Public artifact is the syllabus.

MIT Media Lab · MAS.664 · Spring 2026

AI Studio: AI Agents and Agentic Web

Ramesh Raskar

A studio course (cross-listed with Sloan and EECS) where teams build autonomous agents that plan, coordinate and execute workflows across distributed web systems, in a venture-style format with outside mentors. Light on mechanism, heavy on shipping and on the "agentic web" thesis. Earlier semesters ran as AI Venture Studio and AI for Impact.

University of Virginia · CS 6501 · Spring 2026

Workshop on Building AI Agents

Henry Kautz

A graduate workshop built around actually constructing agents, with an emphasis on small and domain-specific language models and open-weight systems. The same author publishes the interactive Transformer Logit Lens ↗ linked from our Week 9 page.

Carnegie Mellon · 24-880 · Spring 2026

AI Agents for Engineers

Amir Barati Farimani (Mechanical Engineering)

LLMs and agents for engineering work: an applied course for graduate students and advanced undergraduates in engineering rather than computer science, which makes its scoping decisions closer to ours than the CS courses above.

Stanford · CS 146S · Fall 2026 / Fall 2025

The Modern Software Developer

Mihail Eric

Not an agents course in name, but the fullest public treatment of working with coding agents: defining intent, structuring work and coordinating tools so agents can contribute across a real codebase. Slides, readings and assignments are free. Pairs with our Week 2.

Stanford · CS 329T · Autumn

Building and evaluating agentic AI applications

Project-based; 70% project

The course's own framing: a first prototype is easy, and refining it into something trustworthy is the work. Lectures on prompting and RAG, agent architectures, and evaluating and improving agent trustworthiness, with slides and labs posted as the term runs.

Where this course sits

What we do differently, on purpose

Business undergraduates

One prior programming course, not a systems sequence. Every assignment has a code path and a no-code path graded to the same rubric, and the course asks the manager's questions first: who owns control flow, what does a run cost, when is it trusted unattended.

Local first

From Week 5 the agents run on a small model on your own laptop, so there is no API bill, no key to leak, and the unreliability of small models becomes the week's lesson instead of a surprise.

Security and governance carry weight

Evaluation (Week 8), security (Week 10) and agentic commerce (Week 11) are full weeks, and the capstone is graded on an eval harness and a security review, not only on whether the demo runs.
