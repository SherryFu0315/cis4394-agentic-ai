      What this snapshot can tell us

      These are requests and tokens processed through OpenRouter, not model downloads or unique users. A larger token count can reflect more requests, longer inputs, longer outputs, or a combination.

      Grouping models and calculating shares

      Model view keeps every canonical model ID separate. Developer view sums all observed models with the same OpenRouter author prefix: for example, openai/* becomes OpenAI and anthropic/* becomes Anthropic. These are model authors, not the providers hosting inference. Distinct author namespaces remain separate, even when they belong to the same parent company.

      The ranking sums the selected measure over observed rows and sorts from largest to smallest, breaking ties by ID. It shows up to 100 models or developers, 25 per page. Switching the grouping preserves the total and share denominator for the selected country group. Country shares use the selected model’s or developer’s reported total across all groups. All shares describe usage within OpenRouter only.

      Country groups, suppression, and missing observations

      Country refers to the request’s network location, not a person’s residence or nationality. “Suppressed country group” and “Unknown country” remain separate. Suppressed model IDs are excluded from model rankings. Published aggregates are retained without reconstructing hidden countries or models. A reported zero remains zero; an absent observation remains absent.

      Source and snapshot timing

      Source: OpenRouter’s licensed daily token volumes by request country.

      Data are a dated snapshot, not a live feed. Recent figures can be revised; current coverage does not establish complete global AI usage.
